> **TL;DR** — A provider's public page can identify whom to ask. It cannot prove that a specific PCB stack-up, panel, test configuration, schedule or accredited report has been accepted. Carry the exact product requirement into a dated, written response.

The examples below were checked on **29 September 2026** from the providers' own public pages. They are external examples, not selected suppliers for a Synergy product and not endorsements. The figures and service listings may change; check the linked pages again when a real project is quoted.

| Provider's published statement | What it supports | What remains to confirm |
|---|---|---|
| [JLCPCB rigid PCB capabilities](https://jlcpcb.com/capabilities/Capab) list 0.10/0.10 mm minimum trace width/spacing for 1 oz, 1- or 2-layer boards, and a 0.15 mm hole / 0.25 mm via diameter for 2-layer boards. The same page gives extra conditions and cost notes for small holes. | These are published process-screening values for the stated cases. | The actual copper stack-up, finish, drilled/finished hole definition, panel, exceptions, cost, lead time and acceptance of the exact design. These values are **not** default design rules or a waiver of a stricter project target. |
| [TÜV SÜD's EMC lab page](https://www.tuvsud.com/th-th/about-us/tuv-sud-ibp/lab-capabilities/emc-lab-and-chamber) lists 3 m and 10 m EMC chambers and includes CISPR 32 among standards for multimedia equipment. | A public reason to ask about a possible EMC test route. | The correct entity and location, product category, exact standard edition and test method, chamber availability, report type, quotation and whether the requested test is inside the **specific laboratory's current accredited scope**. The page's accreditation sentence names TÜV SÜD PSB under the Singapore scheme; it must not be read as proof that every service on this Thailand page is accredited at a Thai site. |

## Turn the page into a decision-ready request

For fabrication, attach the exact revision, Gerber or native design view, stack-up, copper weight, finish, quantity and an image of each questioned feature. Use the [supplier capability handoff]({% post_url 2026-09-29-supplier-capability-handoff %}) and preserve the provider's written answer and conditions. If the answer changes a drawing or release note, recheck the controlled package.

For a lab, send the EUT model and configuration, market and requirement source, operating modes, cables, loads, power arrangement and desired report claim. Use the [pre-compliance intake]({% post_url 2026-09-29-precompliance-test-intake %}). Ask the laboratory to identify the legal entity, site, method and accreditation scope for the proposed test before describing any report as accredited or suitable for certification.

The public-page review is **research only**. Neither provider has been asked to accept a particular project in this article. There is no supplier approval, lab booking, measured result or certification claim.
