> **TL;DR** — Run the digital record alongside the existing control, then reduce routine paper only after reconciliation, exception handling and recovery work in the real receiving area. Set the schedule from observed readiness, not a generic number of weeks.

This is a proposed rollout sequence for GR and IQC. The earlier version of this article presented unverified durations, adoption percentages and interface thresholds as universal facts. Those are withdrawn. Establish a current baseline and agree on project-specific targets before making a business case.

## Gate 1 — Parallel records and reconciliation

Map each paper action to a digital record and identify the authoritative source for PO, receipt, inspection and stock status. Pilot with a bounded product or supplier set. Reconcile paper and digital records daily for the pilot scope, including partial receipts, missing PO references, rejected lots and corrections. Capture mismatches with an owner and resolution.

**Move forward when:** users can retrieve the correct record and its evidence, the same material is not counted twice, holds remain effective, and the responsible owners accept the observed error and recovery process. A percentage recorded without its numerator, denominator and sample period is not an adoption result.

## Gate 2 — Digital primary, paper on demand

Use the digital GR and IQC records as the working source for the pilot scope. Keep an approved way to print or capture paper when the customer, regulation, process or outage requires it. Test the form at the dock and inspection station with real labels, gloves, lighting, devices and network conditions. Preserve inspection notes and attachments without forcing staff to retype source fields.

**Move forward when:** receipt, IQC and stock states stay distinct; the user sees a confirmed save and record number; exceptions can be processed without an informal spreadsheet; and supervisors can review the record and its change history. Time the actual workflow if speed is part of the decision.

## Gate 3 — Controlled routine use

Stop routine paper creation only for the scope that passed Gate 2. Keep the approved outage procedure, including temporary identification of material and later reconciliation. Document which products, suppliers or customers still require physical records. Train the owner who will maintain the form, user access, inspection plan and reporting.

**Check after rollout:** compare GR, IQC and inventory records for missing links, duplicate postings, delayed entry and unclosed holds. Review exceptions with the process owner. A reduction in paper use is not proof of better traceability unless these checks and the [underlying GR/IQC controls]({% post_url 2026-05-27-paperless-gr-iqc %}) pass.
