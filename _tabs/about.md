## Syntech Engineering Hub

A practical knowledge base covering NPI, PCBA engineering, circuit design, engineering operations, system integration and an ISO 56001 innovation management system (IMS), built from real work in Thailand's EMS/OEM industry.

### Topics Covered

- **NPI Process** — RFQ costing, BOM lifecycle, stage gates, DFM/DFA
- **Circuit & Electronics** — Power electronics, LED drivers, field failure RCA
- **AI-Assisted Engineering** — Claude Code, Gemini CLI, AI workflow for engineers
- **IMS (ISO 56001) & Process Governance** — controlled documents, revision release, SOP structure, process ownership
- **Engineering Operations** — release, change control, pilot builds, quality records
- **System Integration** — WMS, MRP, MES architecture patterns
